Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Hotaru is a client for Jellyfin servers. It collects no data: the developer runs no server, and the app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

What the app sends, and where

Hotaru talks only to the Jellyfin server you sign in to. It sends that server your sign-in, a device name and identifier so the server can list the session, what you play and how far you got, and what the headset can decode. To find servers it sends a discovery message on your local network. Nothing is sent to the developer, to Meta by the app, or to anyone else.

What your server keeps is decided by whoever runs it and by Jellyfin, not by Hotaru.

What stays on the headset

The server address, your user name and the sign-in token, which is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, plus your settings. Sign out and forget removes the account and ends its session on the server; uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

Network access, to reach your server. Hand tracking and controller models, so you can point and select; this input is used on the headset and never recorded or sent. Passthrough is drawn by the headset itself: Hotaru cannot see or record the camera view.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions go to the issue tracker; the app's full source is on GitHub.
